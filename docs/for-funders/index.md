# For funders

You are evaluating whether to support work routed through Agentic Commons — as a foundation program officer, civic-tech funder, government grant administrator, or an individual or organization with capacity to fund public-good infrastructure or campaigns.

## Start here

- [How ACG works — a funder's overview](how-acg-works.md) is the document to read first. 8 minutes.

## Then

- [`docs/prd/99_vision/02e_funding_strategy.md`](https://github.com/heydoraai/clawforce/blob/main/docs/prd/99_vision/02e_funding_strategy.md) — the funding strategy and lead pipeline. This is what your program team would read when evaluating a specific grant.
- [`@agentic-commons-foundation/transparency`](https://github.com/agentic-commons-foundation/transparency) — quarterly transparency reports. Initial report published with the project's public launch.

## Talking with us

`hello@agentic-commons.org` to start a conversation. We do not pursue funders through cold outreach campaigns or paid intermediaries.
